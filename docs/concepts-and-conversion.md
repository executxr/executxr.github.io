# Experience concepts and enquiries

Public page: `/beleef-uw-verhaal/`, linked from the homepage in EN/NL/FR navigation. Owner authorized direct publication on 27 November 2026. ExecutXR branding only.

- Digital conversation concept: indicative EUR10,000.
- Interactive reconstruction, with Rupelmonde Castle as example: indicative EUR30,000. This is an example budget, not the historical project's price.
- Education/government: up to15% discount on either concept, confirmed in the quote.
- Scope, installation, recurring costs, tax and timing are determined in the quote. No fixed delivery or all-inclusive package is promised.

The native enquiry form submits to existing HubSpot portal25692968/form6025e8f6-452b-407d-b99f-daebe68a4809. Email and name use existing contact fields. Organisation, concept, question and sanitized campaign source are recorded in the submission's message field. Organisation text is not an automatic CRM company association. A submission does not subscribe someone to marketing.

Optional HubSpot visit tracking loads only after opt-in; DNT/GPC disables it. Preferences can be reopened from the footer. Only bounded UTM source/medium/campaign/content values are passed from the query; arbitrary query strings and fragments are excluded. Following a successful form response, opted-in visitors are identified and a virtual `/beleef-uw-verhaal/aanvraag-ontvangen` pageview is recorded. HubSpot form submissions remain the enquiry source of truth. Clicks/visits are not enquiries or signed sales. No Enterprise custom-event entitlement is assumed.

ExecutXR does not currently have Umami deployed. The NimsForest HubSpot connector reads CRM cohorts and emits its normal observation event; this does not imply every website click is an event on the forest bus.

Validation: desktop/mobile layout; mocked HubSpot rejection and success; form contents retained on error; success shown only after accepted response; no analytics script before consent; private query/hash filtering. Provider validation uses an invalid email to avoid creating a contact or triggering owner notifications. Successful live delivery must be verified against an actual submission in HubSpot; do not describe mocked acceptance as a real CRM conversion.
