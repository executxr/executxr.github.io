How we measure

Three questions we hold ourselves to.

Reach and engagement mean little on their own. We measure whether the right people came, whether they came back, and whether they brought someone.

R1

The right visitor

Not more visitors — the ones your story was meant for. Defined before anything is built.

audience fit · cost per contact moment

R2

The returning visitor

A second visit is the honest test of whether the first one meant something.

return rate · occupancy per slot

R3

The referring visitor

When someone brings a colleague, a class or a family, the story travelled without you.

referral share · word of mouth

Last layer

Where XR makes the difference — and where it does not.

The medium comes last. Often it is immersive, because depth and reach meet there: one experience, intense for the visitor and still able to travel between locations. Sometimes the answer is an exhibition, a film, or a table to sit around. We have no stake in one outcome.

Infrastructure
